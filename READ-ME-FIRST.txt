TWL — the TXT record: where to get it, where to paste it
========================================================

Commit: 632cfd8

PART 1 — GETTING THE TXT VALUE FROM NORTHFLANK
----------------------------------------------
1. Log in to Northflank: https://app.northflank.com
2. Open this page directly: https://app.northflank.com/s/account/domains/new
   (or: the project/account menu -> Account settings -> Domains)
3. Click "Add domain".
4. Type exactly:  traderswelfarelogistics.online
   No https://, no www, no trailing slash.
5. Click "Add domain".
6. The entry now appears with a status like "Unverified". Open it (or click the
   verification entry beside it). It shows two things:

     record name     e.g. _northflank-challenge.traderswelfarelogistics.online
     record content  a long random string

   Each has a copy icon. Click both and paste them into Notepad — leave that
   Northflank tab OPEN, you come back to it in Part 3.

PART 2 — PASTING IT INTO NAMECHEAP
----------------------------------
1. Log in at namecheap.com
2. Left menu -> Domain List
3. Find traderswelfarelogistics.online -> click "Manage"
4. Click the "Advanced DNS" tab
5. Click the "ADD NEW RECORD" button
6. Type dropdown -> choose "TXT Record"
7. HOST field: paste the record name, then DELETE THE DOMAIN OFF THE END.
   Namecheap already knows the domain.
       _northflank-challenge.traderswelfarelogistics.online   <- from Northflank
       _northflank-challenge                                  <- what you type
   If Northflank showed just the bare domain, type  @
8. TARGET field: paste the record content EXACTLY.
   - no quotation marks (Namecheap adds them itself)
   - no https://
   - no trailing dot
9. TTL: choose "1 min" (fastest feedback). Lower TTL = faster verification.
10. Click the green checkmark to SAVE. Nothing is saved until you do — this is why
    records sometimes "disappear". Then click "Save all changes" if shown.

PART 3 — TELL NORTHFLANK TO CHECK
---------------------------------
1. Back on the Northflank domains page, find your domain entry.
2. Click "Verify".
3. Usually passes within a minute or two with TTL 1 min. DNS can occasionally take
   longer — if it fails, wait 10 minutes and press Verify again. Nothing is broken.

THE MOST COMMON MISTAKE
-----------------------
Pasting the full record name into Host. You then get:
   _northflank-challenge.traderswelfarelogistics.online.traderswelfarelogistics.online
It never verifies and looks like Northflank is at fault. Strip the domain.

AFTER THE DOMAIN IS VERIFIED (not before)
-----------------------------------------
Add the two records that actually serve the site:
   ADD NEW RECORD -> ALIAS Record  -> Host: @    -> Target: (what Northflank gives for the bare domain)
   ADD NEW RECORD -> CNAME Record  -> Host: www  -> Target: (what Northflank gives for www)

CORRECTION to what I told you last time: I said Namecheap cannot point the bare
domain at a service and you would need a redirect. That was wrong — Namecheap
supports an ALIAS record at @, which is what Northflank's own Namecheap guide uses.
So the bare domain works directly, and there is no need to move to Cloudflare.
(If Namecheap refuses the ALIAS on your account, fall back to CNAME on www plus a
URL Redirect Record on @ to https://www.traderswelfarelogistics.online.)

Then, in the API settings, restart after setting:
   FRONTEND_URL=https://traderswelfarelogistics.online
   CORS_ORIGINS=https://traderswelfarelogistics.online,https://www.traderswelfarelogistics.online
