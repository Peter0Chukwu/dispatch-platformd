WHAT THIS FIXES
---------------
You said the verification screen showed an error. There were two faults, and I
fixed both, plus one more I found on the way.

1. THE LINK WENT TO A PAGE THAT DOES NOT EXIST
   The email said "click here" and the link was /verify-email?token=... but the app
   only ever had /verify. So every click landed on the 404 page, the token was never
   used, and the address could never be confirmed. The link now goes to /verify, and
   /verify-email is kept as an alias so links already in people's inboxes start
   working instead of failing.

2. THE SCREEN SAID "WE SENT YOU A CODE" WHEN NOTHING WAS SENT
   With no SMS or email provider configured, the code was written into the API's log
   file. A customer cannot read that. Worse, the messaging code threw an error when a
   real provider was selected, which turned a sign-up into a server error. Now:
     - SMS goes to Termii for real, email goes to Resend for real;
     - a failed message is reported, never thrown: the account is always created;
     - the verification screen says "we could not send the SMS just now" when that is
       the truth, and the code still works;
     - "Send the link again" now works without signing in — a brand-new customer has
       no session, and before this there was no way for them to ask for it again.

3. CALLING NOW WORKS, AND NO PHONE NUMBER IS INVOLVED
   The Call button could only say "not implemented yet", because masked calling in
   Nigeria needs an account with KYC, a rented pool of numbers and per-minute
   billing — none of it self-serve. But the promise was never "dial a proxy number".
   The promise was "talk without either of you learning the other's number".

   A call inside the app keeps that promise with no number in it at all. Press Call
   on a live delivery; the other person's phone rings in the app; they press Answer
   and you talk. The audio goes straight from one browser to the other and never
   through our server, and nothing is recorded.

   The rules are enforced on the server, not in the browser:
     - only the two people on that delivery may call each other
     - only while the job is live: refused before a rider is assigned and after the
       job is delivered or cancelled — that is the "no contact after the job" rule
     - one call at a time, 45 seconds to answer, and closing the tab hangs up
     - a fresh call id every time, so an old one cannot be reused

TO TURN ON REAL SMS AND EMAIL (both optional, both free to start)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Email — Resend, free to 3,000 a month:
  1. resend.com -> add your domain -> add the DNS records it shows you
  2. API Keys -> create -> copy
  3. In the API settings:  EMAIL_MODE=RESEND
                           RESEND_API_KEY=re_...
                           EMAIL_FROM="TWL <no-reply@yourdomain.com>"

SMS — Termii, roughly N4 per message:
  1. termii.com -> top up a little credit
  2. Request a sender ID (3-11 characters, e.g. TWL). Approval takes a few days.
  3. Ask Termii support, in writing, to enable the DND (transactional) route on your
     account. Without it OTPs are refused or never reach numbers with do-not-disturb
     on. This is the step people miss.
  4. In the API settings:  SMS_MODE=TERMII
                           TERMII_API_KEY=TL...
                           TERMII_SENDER_ID=TWL
                           TERMII_CHANNEL=dnd

How to check it is really on: open /api/v1/health and look at "integrations". It
should read "sms":"TERMII" and "email":"RESEND". If it reads TERMII_NO_KEY or
RESEND_NO_KEY, the mode is switched on but the key is missing — that is the state
that looks configured and sends nothing.

TWO HONEST LIMITS OF IN-APP CALLING
-----------------------------------
- Both people must have the app open. A locked phone gets nothing until push
  notifications (item 4) are added.
- A few networks block direct browser-to-browser audio. When that happens the panel
  says so and points at the message box, which always works. Item 5 fixes it.

HOW TO PROVE IT, IN FIVE MINUTES
--------------------------------
1. Sign in as a customer, book a delivery; accept it as a rider in a second browser
   (or a private window).
2. In the customer window open the delivery, find Contact, press Call.
3. The rider window shows "Customer is calling" with Answer / Decline.
4. Press Answer, allow the microphone, talk. The timer starts when audio connects.
5. Mark the delivery delivered, then press Call again: it refuses. That refusal is
   the feature — contact ends with the job.
