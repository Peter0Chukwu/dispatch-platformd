THE NEW BOOKING FLOW
--------------------
    From  ->  To  ->  Size  ->  Bill

Size is the only package question now. The photo and the description are still there
and are both OPTIONAL — the size is what prices the job.

When the bill appears the customer answers it, and that answer is what riders get:

    Accept the bill   ->  riders are alerted for exactly the bill
    Negotiate         ->  riders are alerted for the customer's number instead

Nothing is charged at this point. Payment happens AFTER a rider has the job, on the
delivery page, as you chose.

WHAT THE RIDER SEES
-------------------
If the customer accepted the bill: the amount, and two buttons — Accept, or Ask for
more. If the customer negotiated: BOTH numbers on the card (the bill, struck through,
and what the customer is offering), and the same two buttons. "Ask for more" opens a
box for the rider's own price and sends it to the customer.

WHAT THE CUSTOMER SEES WHEN A RIDER ASKS FOR MORE
-------------------------------------------------
The delivery page lists the rider, their price, and Accept / Decline.
  Accept  -> that rider gets the job at their price. Everyone else is told it is gone.
  Decline -> the rider's offer stays open at the customer's price. A refusal never
             costs the customer a rider.

THE RULES (enforced on the server, stated in naira)
---------------------------------------------------
  Customer may offer   between half the bill and three times the bill
  Rider may ask        anything above the asking price, up to three times it
  Anything outside     is refused with a plain message, not an error code

Accepting routes through ONE place, so a delivery can never be given to two riders,
and the agreed price is the price charged and the price commission is cut from.

BOTH END UP IN THE CHAT
-----------------------
Accepting sends the customer to /app/deliveries/<id>/chat and the rider to
/rider/deliveries/<id>/chat — the same Contact card as before, chat and in-app
calling, no phone numbers, no inbox list. The thread is for that one job.

>>> BEFORE YOU TEST: THE DATABASE NEEDS A NEW MIGRATION  <<<
-------------------------------------------------------------
This version stores five new money columns on deliveries and five on offers. The API
WILL FAIL on booking until the migration runs. In your Windows terminal:

    cd C:\Users\USER\Documents\dispatch-platform\backend
    set DATABASE_URL=<your production database URL from the hosting dashboard>
    npx prisma migrate deploy
    set DATABASE_URL=

Copy DATABASE_URL from the Northflank dashboard (the API service's environment
variables) — never from chat, and clear it from the terminal afterwards as shown.

A better long-term fix: run `npx prisma migrate deploy` as a Northflank *job* in the
same image at deploy time, so a release can never outrun the schema. Say the word and
I will write that job's exact settings.

TO APPLY THIS BUILD
-------------------
1. Copy the files in this zip over your clone (they keep their paths).
2. Run the migration above.
3. Commit and push, then deploy. The web service needs a rebuild too — the booking
   form, the rider's offer card and the new chat route are all front-end changes.
4. Check the deploy log for: "Email links will point at: ..." (from the last build).

HOW TO TRY IT (demo data)
-------------------------
  Customer   register a new customer at /register, or use any customer account you have
  Staff      admin@dispatch.test / Admin@12345 via /staff
  Rider      demo.rider@dispatch.test / Rider@12345 — go ONLINE on the rider overview

Then: book from any Benin City pickup to a drop-off, choose a size, and on the Bill
step pick "Negotiate" and type a lower number. The rider account (online) sees the
bill struck through and your offer. Ask for more from the rider side, then accept or
decline it on the customer's delivery page.
