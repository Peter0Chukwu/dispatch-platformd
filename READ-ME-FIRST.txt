TRADERS WELFARE — SUGGESTIONS FIXED FOR BENIN CITY
==================================================

YOUR KEY IS GOOD. This patch is about what the key ANSWERS.

WHAT THE TEST SHOWED
  "ring road"    → Ring Road, LAGOS 100242  (wrong state, 300 km away)
  "sapele"       → Sapele, Delta State      (not Benin-Sapele Road)
  "ikpoba hill"  → nothing

  OpenStreetMap has more than one Ring Road in Nigeria and the biggest wins a
  plain search. That would have put a Lagos street on a Benin City booking form.

WHAT CHANGED (commit fe6f3e7)
  1. The platform's own twelve Benin City locations now always rank FIRST when
     the typed words appear in their name. The provider fills in the rest.
  2. A provider result within 200 m of one of ours is dropped — same place,
     said twice.
  3. Every suggestion request carries MAPS_SEARCH_BIAS, default Ring Road, Benin
     City (5.6222,6.3329). Another city = change that one variable.
