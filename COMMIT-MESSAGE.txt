Two things this fixes in the walk-through. First, the Host field: Namecheap wants
the part before the domain, and pasting Northflank's full record name there
silently produces a doubled domain that never verifies — the most common way this
step fails, and it now says so in bold. Second, a correction: Northflank's own
Namecheap guide documents an ALIAS record on @, which is Namecheap's CNAME
flattening. That means the bare domain can point straight at the app and the
redirect-record workaround I suggested last time is unnecessary. The fallback
stays documented for accounts where ALIAS is refused.

Also recorded: paste the target verbatim (no scheme, no quotes, no trailing dot),
TTL 1 minute while verifying, and the green checkmark — Namecheap does not save
an unsaved row, which reads as a record that vanished.
