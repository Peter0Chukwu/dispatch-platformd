maps: our own Benin City locations outrank the geocoder's idea of relevance

Tested the new key against real queries for Benin City. The roads are there, but a
plain provider search answers the wrong question:

  "ring road"  → Ring Road, LAGOS 100242 (a different state, 300 km away)
  "sapele"     → Sapele, Delta State before Benin-Sapele Road
  "ikpoba hill"→ nothing

That is not a Geoapify fault; OpenStreetMap simply has more Ring Roads in Nigeria
than ours, and the biggest one wins. A courier platform cannot ship a booking form
that offers a street in Lagos to someone sending a parcel across Benin City.

So autocomplete() now merges: our twelve surveyed Benin City locations whose NAME
matches what was typed come first, then the provider fills the rest, and a
provider result within 200 m of one of ours is dropped as the same place said
twice. The local match is strict on purpose — words must appear in the name, not
the address, or "road" would drag in half the table and bury a precise hit.

Every suggestion request also carries MAPS_SEARCH_BIAS (default 5.6222,6.3329 —
Ring Road, Benin City, longitude first) so near results win ties. It is
configuration: another city means changing one variable, not the code.

The Google adapter goes through the same merge, so both providers behave alike.
Test fixtures moved to Sakponba Road — a real Benin City street the platform does
NOT keep locally — so the provider-mapping tests exercise the adapter rather than
the merge. 52 unit tests pass.
