feat(booking): three questions, a bill, and a price both sides can argue with

Booking is now from, to and size. The platform prices it and shows the bill, and the
customer answers it: accept it, or name their own number. Whichever they pick is what
nearby online riders are alerted for — nothing is charged until somebody takes the job.

On the rider's card the two numbers are both visible when they differ: the bill the
customer was given, and what the customer is offering. A rider can take the asking
price or ask for more, and that price goes to the customer, who accepts it (that
rider gets the job at that number) or declines it (the offer stays open at the
asking price, so a refusal never costs a customer a rider).

Acceptance now has exactly one path. A rider tapping Accept and a customer accepting a
rider's price both go through DispatchService.conclude, so 'one winner per delivery',
'commission frozen on the agreed number', 'losing riders told' and 'rider marked
busy' are true once rather than true twice. chargeKobo is replaced with the agreed
amount in that same transaction: the number agreed is the number charged.

Both bands are enforced server-side and explained in naira: a customer may offer
between half and three times the bill, a rider may ask for between one kobo and three
times the asking price. The rounding helper was wrong by a factor of 100 (50 kobo is
not N50) and the test caught it.

Accepting sends both people to the thread for that delivery
(/app/deliveries/:id/chat, /rider/deliveries/:id/chat) — the same ContactCard as
before, no phone numbers, still no inbox.
